About

Background

Physics, astronomy, and the grammar of physical law.

I am a student of physics and astronomy based in Chattogram, Bangladesh, currently completing my science curriculum at Chittagong College. My work sits at the intersection of theoretical physics, celestial mechanics, and mathematical modeling — domains I approach with both formal rigor and genuine wonder.

My interest in physics is not primarily competitive, though I have been fortunate to represent Bangladesh and place among the top students internationally. What draws me deeper is the unreasonable elegance of physical law: the way a differential equation can encode the arc of a skipping stone, or an orbital resonance can hold a moon in place for billions of years.

I am an independent researcher and writer. My published article on underdamped motion connects formal analysis with physical intuition. I aim to continue this work — pursuing mentored research, writing with rigour, and contributing to the community of serious young scientists in Bangladesh and beyond.

Outside research, I organize academic programming for our college physics and astronomy club, contribute to science outreach through EcoAstroBD (IAU-OAD supported), and participate in distributed citizen science via Zooniverse.
