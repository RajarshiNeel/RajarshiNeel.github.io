About

I am a student of physics and astronomy based in Chattogram, Bangladesh, currently completing my science curriculum at Chittagong College. My work lives at the intersection of theoretical physics, celestial mechanics, and mathematical modeling — domains I approach with both formal rigor and genuine wonder.

My interest in physics is not primarily competitive, though I have been fortunate to represent Bangladesh and place among the top students internationally. What draws me deeper is the unreasonable elegance of physical law: the way a differential equation can encode the arc of a skipping stone, or an orbital resonance can hold a moon in place for billions of years.

I am an independent researcher and writer. My published article on underdamped motion connects formal analysis with physical intuition in a way I hope makes the mathematics feel inevitable rather than imposed. I aim to continue this work — building computational models, pursuing mentored research, and contributing to the community of serious young scientists in Bangladesh and beyond.

Outside research, I organize academic programming for our college physics and astronomy club, contribute to science outreach through EcoAstroBD, and participate in distributed citizen science through Zooniverse.

A Note on Research Collaboration

I am actively seeking mentored research opportunities in theoretical astrophysics, orbital mechanics, or computational physics — either remotely or at institutions in South or Southeast Asia. If your work touches on these areas, I would welcome a conversation.

Research Interests

⊙
Celestial Mechanics & Orbital Dynamics

The mathematics of gravitational systems — from two-body orbits to resonance, perturbation theory, and the long-term stability of planetary configurations. Especially interested in eclipse geometry, transit timing, and the chaotic boundaries of orbital stability.

∿
Dynamical Systems & Oscillatory Motion

Nonlinear dynamics, damped and driven oscillators, resonance phenomena, and the geometry of phase space. My published work on underdamped motion grew from this fascination with the deep connection between physical behavior and mathematical structure.

◈
Computational Physics & Simulation

Numerical methods for physical systems — integrating equations of motion, simulating N-body problems, and building models that make theoretical predictions testable and visible. I believe computation is the third language of modern physics, alongside experiment and theory.

✦
Astrophysics & Observational Astronomy

Stellar structure, variable stars, the physics of formation and death. I approach observational questions analytically — asking what the data constrains and what the theory predicts. Interested especially in photometric analysis and light-curve modeling.

∂
Mathematical Physics & Modeling

The formal structures underlying physical law — Lagrangian and Hamiltonian mechanics, symmetry and conservation, the geometry of configuration space. I am drawn to problems where mathematical elegance and physical insight arrive together.

◉
Science Communication & Pedagogy

Making rigorous physics accessible without sacrificing depth. I write expository articles, lead problem-solving sessions, and design curricula for our college club — driven by the belief that genuine understanding is more valuable than rote technique.

Projects & Investigations

Analytical Mechanics · Published
The Physics of a Skipping Stone

An independent article developing an analytical treatment of underdamped oscillatory motion through the physical intuition of stone-skipping. Connects damping coefficients, energy dissipation, and oscillatory frequency in a unified framework accessible to advanced secondary students.

LaTeX Analytical Mechanics Oscillatory Dynamics

Computational Physics · In Development
N-Body Orbital Integrator

A Python-based numerical integrator for simulating N-body gravitational systems using Runge-Kutta and symplectic methods. Intended to model orbital resonances, test stability bounds, and visualize phase-space trajectories in multi-planet configurations.

Python NumPy / SciPy Matplotlib Orbital Mechanics
In Development
Eclipse Geometry · Observational
Eclipse Geometry and Shadow Geometry

An independent investigation into the analytical geometry of solar and lunar eclipse prediction: constructing the umbral/penumbral cone geometry from first principles, deriving eclipse conditions from orbital parameters, and connecting geometric models to observational data.

Geometry Orbital Parameters Observational Astronomy
